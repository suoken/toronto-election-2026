TORONTO - Today, Bradford for Mayor launched its first paid advertising buy of the 2026 mayoral campaign. The ad focuses on a typical Toronto morning: a routine every parent in this city recognizes. It is also Brad Bradford's actual life: husband, father of two young kids, city councillor for Beaches - East York, and a former urban planner who knows how cities should be built and how City Hall should work.

The core of the ad is what has driven this campaign from the start: that City Hall has conditioned Torontonians to accept less. Toronto can and should be a city we can be proud of again, and people deserve a City Hall that works as hard as they do, not one that asks them to accept disorder, congestion, and crime as the new normal.

“Every morning, parents across Toronto share a similar experience. Trying to get everyone out the door on time, finding shoes, packing lunches, all while hoping the traffic is flowing, the TTC is running, and there are no delays.

“Torontonians show up for each other. They are working hard, giving this city their time, their talent, and their taxes every single day. They keep their end of the bargain. City Hall has to start delivering for them.

“That is why I am running. I want my daughters to grow up in a city we can be proud of. Toronto works because Torontonians make it work. They deserve a City Hall that does the same.”

Brad Bradford

The advertising buy supports the campaign's three priorities for the city: crime, congestion, and cost of living. The ad will run on connected TV and digital platforms in the coming weeks.

For a non-incumbent running for Mayor of Toronto, paid advertising is not a nice-to-have. It is decisive. The mayoral spending cap is roughly half the per-voter ceiling for a provincial or federal candidate, stretched across a campaign window five to six times as long as a federal or provincial writ. An incumbent enjoys daily free amplification from the platform of City Hall: press conferences, announcements, ribbon cuttings, City of Toronto channels. A challenger does not have that platform.

Every dollar a non-incumbent spends has to work harder, and every buy has to do more than it would in any peer race in North America. That is why this campaign is investing in disciplined paid amplification between now and October 26. Voters can watch the ad on Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook.

TORONTO — Today, over 200 women gathered with Oscar-winning filmmaker and activist Sarah Polley and Mayor Olivia Chow for a "Women Canvassing" Day of Action, mobilizing voters across Toronto to support Chow’s re-election campaign.

“This election feels crucial to the future of Toronto and its potential to be a place where future generations can build a good life. From affordable housing to transit, Olivia Chow has been a consistent advocate for those who have been overlooked and ignored by so many governments,” said Sarah Polley. “I'm so proud to stand with all of these people today to support re-electing Olivia Chow as mayor.”

The high canvass turnout reflects the campaign’s growing grassroots momentum and brings into sharp focus what is at stake for women and families in this election. From protecting essential city services like childcare, public transit, and libraries to standing strong against Brad Bradford’s backwards agenda, which would cut the services women and their families depend on.

"When women come together to fight for their communities, they transform our city," said Olivia Chow. "I am so inspired by the hundreds of women who stepped up today to talk to their neighbours. Together, we can protect the public services families rely on every day and build a Toronto that is more caring, affordable, and safer for everyone."

Under Chow’s leadership, Toronto has prioritized community safety, expanded public services, and ensured women and diverse communities have a direct voice in city decisions.

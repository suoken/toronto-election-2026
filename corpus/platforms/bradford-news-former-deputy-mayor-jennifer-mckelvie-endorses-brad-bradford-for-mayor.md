Title: Former Deputy Mayor, Jennifer McKelvie Endorses Brad Bradford for Mayor
Published: 2026-09-09
Issue category: Toronto Mayoral Campaign 2026

Toronto ON, Current Member of Parliament and former Deputy Mayor and Toronto City Councillor Jennifer McKelvie today announced her support for Brad Bradford’s candidacy for Mayor of Toronto.

“[...] I think you have that vision that Toronto needs right now. I think you have all the right qualities that we need. You’re smart, you work hard and you care; and we are a city that can have nice things. I know that you can deliver on transit, on housing, and the priorities that I cared about most, the environment. So, I’m really looking forward to seeing you as the Mayor of Toronto,” said McKelvie.

McKelvie and Bradford were colleagues at City Hall, both first elected in 2018. They served as Chair of the Infrastructure and Environment Committee and the Chair of Planning and Housing Committee respectively.

"Toronto isn't broken. It's stuck. Stuck paying more, stuck getting less," said Bradford. "I am thrilled to have the support and endorsement of my friend Jennifer McKelvie, someone who served the great people of Scarborough admirably for a number of years as councillor, and delivered great things for Toronto as Deputy Mayor."

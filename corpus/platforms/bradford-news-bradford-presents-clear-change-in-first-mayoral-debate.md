Title: BRADFORD PRESENTS CLEAR CHANGE IN FIRST MAYORAL DEBATE
Published: 2026-09-15
Issue category: Toronto Mayoral Campaign 2026

TORONTO, ON - Brad Bradford tonight presented a clear alternative to Mayor Olivia Chow, marking himself as the only candidate on the debate stage that can bring change to the City of Toronto.

“The choice for Torontonians was made clear in tonight's debate. Four more years of paying more, and getting less; or real change with real results,” said Bradford. “Our campaign has been focused on the things that matter most to residents of this city: Tackling crime, lowering costs, and alleviating the crippling congestion that has left this city stuck.”

“At a time when Toronto families are struggling to cover their monthly bills, Toronto needs real change and real results to tackle the rising cost of living in this city. Only our campaign can bring that change, giving Toronto a real choice this election.”

While Torontonians struggle with these costs, Mayor Chow raised property taxes by nearly 20% in just three years. She has since asked Torontonians to believe her this time, and committed this campaign to raise property taxes “around inflation.”

Bradford highlighted key commitments tonight, including:

-
Building more homes in Toronto by getting bureaucracy out of the way

-
Making transit more reliable and safe

-
Mandatory treatment for those struggling with drug addictions

-
Platform Edge Doors on the TTC subway.

-
Hiring 1,000 Officers in his first term as Mayor.

"Toronto is stuck paying more and getting less," said Bradford. "There's more the Mayor should be doing to lower costs for families, to ease congestion on our roadways and in our transit; instead, she's asking Torontonians for 4 more years to finish a job she never started. That's the choice in this election. Olivia Chow is asking you to pay more and settle for less. I'm asking Torontonians to choose change.”

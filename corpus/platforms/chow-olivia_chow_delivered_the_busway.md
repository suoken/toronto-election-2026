TORONTO (September 30, 2026) — Olivia Chow delivered on her promise to Scarborough, the Scarborough Busway opened for service today - and it’s more than six months ahead of schedule.

"When the Scarborough RT derailed, people in Scarborough were left stuck in traffic, and their commutes got longer and harder," said Chow. "Some people said a busway was too expensive. I made a choice to invest in Scarborough, not neglect it like others have. Starting today, transit riders will get faster bus commutes, and next we’re going to lower TTC fares and build more rapid transit in Scarborough. Let’s keep moving forward, not backwards.”

After being extended 10 years past its lifespan, the Scarborough RT derailed. Brad Bradford served as a TTC Commissioner when the TTC stopped doing preventive maintenance on the Scarborough RT, which was found by external consultants to be a contributing factor to the derailment. With Bradford at the TTC, the Scarborough RT deteriorated and eventually derailed.

The new bus-only corridor gives riders a faster connection between Scarborough Centre and Kennedy stations, saving riders an hour per week. It is expected to serve more than 100,000 riders each week, with multiple TTC routes using the corridor.

TORONTO — Olivia Chow took the stage tonight at the Scarborough Mayoral Debate held at the University of Toronto’s Scarborough campus with a clear message: Scarborough needs someone they can trust.

Scarborough needs a leader they can rely on. Olivia Chow shared her vision for a more caring, more affordable and safer city. She showed how to build on the progress that Toronto has made over the past three years - by investing in youth safety programs, public transit and housing.

Her record for Scarborough is clear: opening the Scarborough busway, making the Scarborough East Rapid Transit Line our city’s top transit priority, building thousands of new units of housing in Scarborough, and investing in community services.

Scarborough leaders are supporting Olivia Chow

Tonight, immediately following the debate, Chow was endorsed by David Soknacki, former Toronto Budget Chief and Scarborough City Councillor (see here).

Scarborough’s leaders have worked with Olivia Chow and know she delivers. She has also been endorsed by:

-

Gary Anandasangaree, Liberal MP for Scarborough—Guildwood—Rouge Park

-

Jean Yip, Liberal MP for Scarborough—Agincourt

-

Salma Zahid, Liberal MP for Scarborough Centre—Don Valley East

-

Doly Begum, Liberal MP for Scarborough Southwest

-

Fatima Shaban, NDP MPP for Scarborough Southwest

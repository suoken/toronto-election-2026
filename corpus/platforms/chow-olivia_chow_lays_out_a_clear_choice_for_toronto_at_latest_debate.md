TORONTO (October 6, 2026) — Olivia Chow took the stage tonight at the Toronto Star and United Way’s debate with a clear message: this election is a choice between moving forward and the risk of going backwards.

Olivia Chow shared her vision for a more caring, more affordable and safer city, one that builds on the progress Toronto has made over the past three years.

But there's more to do, and Chow wants to keep going. If re-elected, she'll freeze TTC fares for a fourth year and introduce a 10-week fare cap for riders. She already made the school morning meal program universal. Next, she'll do the same for school lunches. She'll also crack down on bad landlords. Chow has delivered, and now she's ready to go further.

Councillor Bradford will say anything to get elected, but his schemes mean cuts. Cuts that hurt working people and make life more expensive. Bradford sat on the Budget Committee that left the City with a $2 billion budget hole. He chaired the Planning and Housing Committee while approvals dragged on for more than 1,000 days. And during his time on the TTC board, fares went up and service was cut.

Torontonians have seen where that road leads. That's what's at stake in this election.

Olivia Chow will keep sharing her vision for a more caring, more affordable and safer city, Toronto for all over the coming weeks.

Title: BRADFORD CALLS ON MAYOR CHOW TO TAKE HER SEAT ON THE POLICE BOARD
Published: 2026-07-29
Issue category: Crime

Motion before City Council this week asks the Mayor to personally occupy her designated seat on the board that oversees the Toronto Police Service

TORONTO - Toronto City Councillor and candidate for Mayor Brad Bradford has presented a motion for this week’s meeting of City Council calling on Mayor Olivia Chow to personally take her designated seat on the Toronto Police Service Board.

Under provincial law, the Mayor of Toronto is entitled to serve on the Police Service Board, the civilian body that oversees the Toronto Police Service and sets its budget and priorities. Mayor Chow has chosen to delegate that seat to a designate rather than occupy it herself.

“Community safety is job number one of local government,” said Bradford. “When the biggest decisions about policing in this city get made, the Mayor isn’t in the room. She sent someone else. Residents deserve a Mayor who shows up.”

The motion will be considered when City Council meets July 29 to 31. The full text of the motion is available on the City of Toronto website: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.MM43.50

“I’ve already committed that as Mayor, I’ll take that seat personally,” said Bradford. “Leadership is about accountability, and that responsibility should rest with the person voters elect to lead the city. A city that is not safe is not free.”

The motion builds on Bradford’s community safety plan, including his commitment announced on July 23 to hire 1,000 new front line police officers over his first term, with further announcements in the weeks ahead.

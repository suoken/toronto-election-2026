## Meet BRAD BRADFORD

Torontonians give their time, their talent, and their taxes to this city every single day. They deserve a mayor who finally gives that back.

Brad Bradford loves this city. An urban planner, City Councillor, husband, and dad of two, Brad has spent his career on the work of how cities actually get built and how City Hall actually works. He believes Toronto can be the best city in the world to raise a family, build a business, and unlock your potential. That belief is what got him into public service, and it is what gets him out of bed every morning.

#### Why Brad wants to be Mayor

Brad is running for Mayor because Torontonians have been taught to accept less, and he refuses to. He believes we deserve a city we can be proud of again, one that works for the people who pay for it. His campaign is focused on the three things Torontonians struggle with every day: Crime, Congestion, and Cost.

## Here's how we make Toronto the best city in the world

01

### Crime

Public safety will be Brad's first job as mayor. Brad will take his seat on the Toronto Police Service Board that is reserved for the mayor, and hire 1,000 new front-line police officers over his first term. A city that is not safe is not free. He will also crack down on dangerous e-bikes and increase voluntary and mandatory treatment options for people struggling with serious addiction. He will judge success by one test: whether residents in every part of the city feel safe on their streets, on transit and in their homes.

02

### Congestion

Time is the one thing none of us can get back, and the time tax City Hall levies on residents climbs every year. Toronto is stuck in traffic. Brad will get it moving with his comprehensive plan to cut congestion. His top transit priority is getting shovels in the ground on the Eglinton East LRT by 2030. He will remove the Bloor Street West bike lanes in Etobicoke (from South Kingsway to Resurrection Road), protect the rest, and build a citywide cycling network. He will remove the red bus lanes in Scarborough that aren't working, and improve safety and reliability on the subway by building real platform edge doors.

### Cleanliness

Brad will restore the square at Yonge and Dundas as a clean, vibrant public space at the heart of downtown. Visible, uniformed officers will patrol it and other busy public spaces like Union Station.

He will set one standard for lighting and cleanliness across every TTC station, so riders can count on a station that feels clean, bright and safe wherever they get on. No parent should have to think twice before their kids get on the subway. The people of this city deserve parks, streets and stations that are cared for the way they used to be.

05

### Compete

Brad believes Toronto's best days are ahead, but only if the next generation can afford to build a life and a career here. Right now, too many of them are leaving. On his first day in office, Brad will launch the Toronto Youth Employment Challenge to create 15,000 permanent full-time jobs for young people. He will make it easier to register for recreation programs and support Toronto manufacturers with property tax relief.

Brad will also modernize Toronto's budget and set strong fiscal guardrails to make Toronto the most fiscally responsible major government in North America. Toronto isn't broken. It's stuck. Change is within our grasp.

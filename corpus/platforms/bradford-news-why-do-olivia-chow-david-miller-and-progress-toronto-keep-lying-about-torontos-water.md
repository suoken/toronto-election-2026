Title: WHY DO OLIVIA CHOW, DAVID MILLER AND PROGRESS TORONTO KEEP LYING ABOUT TORONTO'S WATER?
Published: 2026-10-22
Issue category: Toronto Mayoral Campaign 2026

Bradford for Mayor spokesperson Laryssa Waler issued the following statement:

“With 4 days to go, Olivia Chow, David Miller and Progress Toronto are still lying to the people of this city that Brad Bradford would privatize Toronto's water. They've been told it's false. They've been shown the law, the Water and Wastewater Public Corporations Act, 2025. They keep saying it anyway. So the question is no longer whether the claim is true. It's why these people and organizations who know better keep repeating something they know is not.

The answer: it's dirty politics.

“Brad's plan is a public water utility, owned 100% by the City of Toronto. It's the same model Toronto Hydro has run under since 1999, and the model Edmonton, Windsor, Kingston and Halifax use for water today. Nobody calls Toronto Hydro privatized. Nobody calls Halifax Water privatized. The plan cuts every water bill in this city by 25%, about $300 a year for the average household, with no application and not one service cut. Every Toronto Water worker keeps the same job under the same contract.

“Don't just take the campaign's word for it:

“Brad Bradford is right: the statutory framework does not allow the Mayor of Toronto to sell any part of Toronto's water system. Any suggestion otherwise is politics not law.”

-
Walied Soliman, Toronto lawyer

“The provincial legislation literally does not allow privatization. It must stay public. And, the entire reasons for this program is to make housing and economic development costs lower to make those very things more affordable for people.”

-
Post on X (Twitter), Cam Guthrie, Mayor of Guelph, October 22, 2026

“The province consulted on many of these measures before introducing the Bill, and many reflect AMO's feedback, including ensuring water and wastewater corporations remain publicly owned.”

-
Association of Municipalities of Ontario, Statement from May 14, 2026

“A leading Toronto lawyer, a sitting mayor and the organization that speaks for Ontario's municipalities, all saying the same thing. The law does not allow privatization. Toronto's water stays public. Period.

“So why do Olivia Chow, David Miller and Progress Toronto keep saying otherwise? Because NDP Mayor Chow has nothing to say about her own record. The province has never given this city more money, and she still raised property taxes nearly 20%, the largest increase in the city's history. If she's re-elected, she'll raise them another 20% at least. Food bank visits are up 340% since 2019, 4.1 million visits last year alone. And Olivia Chow stood on the debate stage and declared that Toronto has reached its full potential. Brad rejects that. We're not even close.

Title: Scarborough deserves amazing transit - Olivia Chow for Mayor of Toronto

Scarborough deserves amazing transit

Olivia Chow is delivering better transit across the city:

- Faster LRTs and streetcars

- Better bus service

- Three years of frozen TTC fares – plus a fare cap for frequent riders

And now, the Scarborough Busway is opening six months early.

A campaign commitment Olivia made in 2023 is becoming reality – giving 120,000 riders every week a faster commute and saving them more than an hour.

Next up: the Scarborough East LRT.

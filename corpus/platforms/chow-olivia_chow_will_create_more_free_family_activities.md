Olivia Chow will create more free family activities

More free access to arts and culture, more kids library programming

TORONTO - An average day out with your family can cost hundreds of dollars, Olivia Chow will make it easier to take advantage of what our city has to offer, for free.

By expanding the Museum and Attractions Pass so 1000 more family passes are available each month, and adding children' s programming in libraries every weekend, Olivia Chow will provide more free places to take the kids.

“Taking the family out to a museum can cost you over $100 in admission tickets. It’s just so expensive. I will make it much easier for families to take advantage of what Toronto has to offer, for free,” said Chow. “In an affordability crunch, we can move forward by building on what’s working. We can expand our existing programs to help more families access our city’s great arts, culture and education attractions, for free.

Chow’s plan to give families more free things to do:

-

Expand Toronto’s Museum and Attractions Pass: Everyone deserves to access our city’s artistic and cultural experiences and attractions. For too many families, the cost is prohibitive. Olivia will work with cultural institutions and the Toronto Public Library Foundation to expand the Museum and Attractions Pass program, so 1,000 more free family passes are available - to more attractions - each month.

-

All-weekend library kids’ programming at all branches: As Mayor, Olivia opened all 100 library branches 7 days a week. She will go further and deliver free high-quality kids’ programs in all branches on weekends, so there is always somewhere affordable and engaging for families to go. For example, this Sunday, this would add programs at 36 libraries across the city.

Example of the Museum and Attractions pass:A family of four would have to spend over $90 to visit the ROM on a weekend. By using the TPL’s Museum and Attractions Pass, it’s free. The challenge: there are only a certain number of passes available each month. Chow will work with the ROM and TPL Foundation to expand it so more families can take advantage.

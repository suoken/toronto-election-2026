TORONTO (September 22, 2026) — Olivia Chow took the debate stage tonight with a clear message: We’ve made practical progress over the past three years, but we’re just getting started. Let’s keep moving forward.

Olivia Chow talked about her time as mayor, bringing forward affordability measures like freezing TTC fares while boosting service, expanding school food programs to 330,000 kids, and ending over a decade of stalled affordable housing construction. She’s committed to building on this progress to create a more affordable city for everyone.

On the City’s finances, she highlighted fixing the $2 billion budget hole left by Councillor Bradford, who sat on the Budget Committee in the previous administration. Her New Deal helped to fix the budget without cutting services people need. Chow fixed Bradford’s financial mess and put the City back onto a sustainable path.

On housing, she talked about speeding up approvals by 80%. Under Councillor Bradford as Chair of the Planning and Housing committee, Toronto’s approval times were over 1000 days. She fixed Bradford’s mess and now it's 83 days.

On moving around the city, she highlighted investments to increase TTC service while keeping fares low. Under Councillor Bradford’s time on the TTC board, fares went up and service was cut. She’s fixing Bradford’s mess there too. She pushed back on Chris Alexander’s plan to increase costs for families that would see surge pricing and higher fares come to the TTC.

Olivia Chow will keep sharing her vision for a more affordable, safer, and more caring Toronto for all over the coming weeks.

Olivia Chow Receives LiUNA 183 Endorsement

Olivia Chow is proud to accept the endorsement of the Labourers’ International Union of North America (LiUNA) Local 183, including hundreds of LiUNA workers who attended last night’s campaign launch rally.

TORONTO — “Olivia Chow is proud to accept the endorsement of the Labourers’ International Union of North America (LiUNA) Local 183, including hundreds of LiUNA workers who attended last night’s campaign launch rally.

LiUNA Local 183 is the largest local union representing more than 70,000 workers from across the Greater Toronto Area, with members in construction, waste management, building service, maintenance, healthcare, and non-construction trades. As the largest construction union in Toronto, they are responsible for building affordable housing and infrastructure our city needs including hundreds of thousands of homes which Olivia Chow has unlocked as Mayor.

LiUNA previously endorsed Ana Bailão in the 2023 Toronto Mayoral By-Election and Doug Ford in the 2025 Provincial Election. The union’s support for Olivia Chow’s 2026 re-election campaign speaks to the broad coalition of workers and residents supporting the pragmatic approach that Olivia Chow brought to city hall as mayor and vision to keep moving forward to build a caring, affordable and safer city.”

Full Remarks from Jack Oliveira, Business Manager, LiUNA 183:

Our members have helped build Toronto for generations. We have built the roads people travel, the transit systems communities depend on, the homes families live in, and the neighbourhoods that make this city one of the best places to live and work.

Throughout her time as Mayor, Olivia Chow has demonstrated a strong commitment to addressing our housing challenges and improving affordability for everyone who calls Toronto home. Under her leadership, the City has expanded incentives for purpose-built rental housing, helping advance thousands of new rental and affordable homes.

As the trade war continues and uncertainty grows, we need a leader who we can trust to look out for the people of Toronto. Olivia has worked collaboratively with provincial and federal partners, as well as business leaders, to secure significant investments in housing and the infrastructure needed to support growth and create good jobs and prosperity in our city.

At the same time, Mayor Chow has delivered on other key priorities for Toronto residents. The City has frozen TTC fares for three consecutive years, completed Phase 2 of the Gardiner Expressway rehabilitation ahead of schedule, expanded transit service, and continued investing in the roads, water systems, transit networks, and community services that Torontonians rely on every day.

Mayor Chow has proven herself to be a competent leader who gets things done. Her commitment to building housing, investing in critical infrastructure, and making Toronto a more affordable and livable city reflects the values and priorities of our members.

That is why LiUNA Local 183 is proud to endorse Olivia Chow for re-election as Mayor of Toronto.

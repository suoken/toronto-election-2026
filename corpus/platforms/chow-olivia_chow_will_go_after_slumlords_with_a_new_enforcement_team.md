TORONTO (September 25, 2026) – Olivia Chow will crack down on slumlords with new dedicated enforcement.

Chow’s Bad Landlords Task Force will lead an aggressive, proactive enforcement effort directed at problem landlords and buildings. This builds forward on Olivia Chow’s work at 500 Dawes.

“Half of Torontonians rent their homes, they deserve safe and healthy places to live. There are too many slumlords who won’t do the basics, they’ve been getting away with it for too long,” said Chow.

“So far, we’ve supported renters by banning renovictions and investing in more inspectors, but we need to keep moving forward. We can get tough on the bad landlords that make life harder for people in our city,” said Chow.

The new team will consist of enforcement officers and lawyers with a focussed, clear mandate to pursue the worst buildings and landlords, with proactive inspections and issuing court orders for repairs. For landlords that refuse, Chow will ramp up Remedial Action: the City making the repairs and billing the landlord.

“I saw the living conditions at 500 Dawes. Moldy crumbling walls, pests in people’s clothing, and an indifferent landlord. This has to end. Everywhere in our city,” said Chow. “We will take action. I am putting slumlords on notice. If you own even one problem building, we will take action in every building in your portfolio. People deserve to live in dignity, in safe and healthy homes.”

Over her term as mayor, Olivia Chow has stood up for renters. She passed a Renovictions By-law, doubled funding to programs that prevent evictions, like the Rent Bank, doubled the number of RentSafe Officers inspecting buildings, and used the City’s powers of Remedial Action for the first time at 500 Dawes.

TORONTO (October 11, 2026) —- On the last day of advance polls, Olivia Chow spoke with campaign volunteers and voters about the choice facing Torontonians in this election.

“This election is a simple choice. We can keep moving forward by lowering grocery bills and transit fares, and building more housing people can afford. Or we can go backwards with my opponent’s agenda to cut services people rely on, like transit and libraries,” said Chow. “Today is the last day of Advance Polls. Go vote, make your voice heard, and let’s build a more caring, more affordable and safer city. Let’s keep moving forward, together.”

As families sit down at the Thanksgiving table, Chow spoke with voters about how her plan would help to build a more affordable city:

-

Lowering grocery bills - More affordable local grocery stores, feeding kids in schools, and stopping big grocery chains from price gouging

-

Lowering transit fares - 10 rides per week then free, while investing in better service and freezing fares

-

Protecting Renters - cracking down on bad landlords and making sure apartments are repaired

-

Building more homes - Building more thousands more homes people can afford and creating 40,000 more jobs

-

Supporting seniors so they can age at home - More homemakers services, mobile health clinics and social opportunities.

Advance polls are open until 7pm today to cast your ballot. Olivia Chow is #9 on the ballot. Residents who want to volunteer with the campaign can sign up at www.OliviaChow.ca.

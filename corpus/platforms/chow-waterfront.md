Hands Off Our Waterfront

Stop the Provincial Land Grab. Give Toronto Our Waterfront Back.

Toronto stopped the airport expansion—but the fight isn't over.

The Province is still holding onto Little Norway, the Toronto Islands, and other City waterfront lands it seized to make way for an expansion that the Toronto Port Authority (TPA) kept hidden from the public.

Now that the expansion is dead, there's no excuse to keep Toronto's land. As Mayor, Olivia Chow has tabled a motion to return these lands to the City so we can build the waterfront Toronto deserves.

Support Olivia's motion and add your name to demand the Province return our waterfront.

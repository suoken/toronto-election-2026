Support Olivia’s plan to lower TTC costs

As Mayor, Olivia Chow is making transit more affordable for Toronto riders.

Starting this September, fare capping means you pay as you go. Hit 47 rides in a month, and you won’t pay for another ride.

But Olivia isn’t stopping there.

She has a plan to bring that cap down to 40 rides in 2027.

40 rides. Then you ride free for the rest of the month.

That means more money in your pocket – and one less monthly expense to worry about.

Olivia is on a mission to fight the rising cost of living in Toronto.

We need to work together to make it happen. Without public support, we risk going back to rising fares and worse service.

Add your name to support a 40-ride fare cap in 2027.

SCARBOROUGH (October 13, 2026) - Olivia Chow will lower transit costs and build the Scarborough East LRT for Scarborough residents.

Just weeks ago, Chow delivered the new busway for Scarborough, which had 60,000 riders on its first day, and the first month of fare capping was a huge success: nearly 60,000 TTC riders got free rides in September.

She spoke to transit riders grabbing a coffee at Ethio Natural Coffee, around the corner from Victoria Park station.

“I built the busway because I am a mayor who stands up for Scarborough. Next I will build the Scarborough East LRT and make transit much more affordable. I have already set aside the downpayment to build the LRT, shovels will hit the ground in my next term. Re-elect me to keep moving forward and get it done," said Chow.

“For years, my Conservative opponent cut TTC service and raised fares, which left Scarborough behind. He was on the TTC board when they cut maintenance for the Scarborough RT and then it derailed. We can’t go backwards to the days of cuts and poor service. We know it does not work. Let’s keep moving forward, investing in better transit for Scarborough and making life more affordable,” said Chow.

Olivia Chow will deliver for Scarborough transit riders:

-

Lower transit costs: 10 trips then the TTC is free, each week. Lowering the cost of transit can save Scarborough TTC riders hundreds of dollars a year while reducing congestion.

-

Building the Scarborough East LRT: Chow will get shovels in the ground next term. She has already saved nearly $400 million for a downpayment, and she has expedited design and engineering work. Chow has made it the City’s top transit priority.

-

Continuing to invest in more frequent bus service for Scarborough. Throughout her time as mayor, Chow has invested in increasing TTC service levels in Scarborough. She will never cut transit.

-

Keeping her promise to build Scarborough Transit: Chow stood up for Scarborough after the SRT derailed and built the Busway. It’s open and saves 100,000 Scarborough transit riders an hour per week.

-

New subway cars: Chow is buying new state-of-the-art subway trains for Line 2, which will serve the new Scarborough Subway when it opens, giving riders a reliable ride.

BACKGROUND

Building the Scarborough East Rapid Transit Line

-

Chow has made it the City’s top transit priority, has put aside nearly $400 million as a downpayment for the City’s share of funding (approx $1 billion). She also moved more money to expedite the engineers and design work: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.EX30.11

-

Chow has raised the LRT with Provincial and Federal governments as she worked to secure their commitments to funding one-third each

Scarborough Busway

-

In 2023, Olivia Chow committed to building the Scarborough Busway. She kept her commitment to Scarborough transit riders and the Busway opened in September.

-

The busway will save about 100,000 daily commuters an hour per week, across seven or more bus routes. The Kennedy and Midland on-street temporary red lanes will be removed now that the Busway is open.

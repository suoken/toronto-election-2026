Title: Olivia Chow for Mayor of Toronto

Toronto,

Olivia Chow is running for re-election. Let’s build a more affordable, safe and caring city.

In just three years as our Mayor, she’s shown what’s possible. School meals for kids, transit fares frozen, libraries open seven days a week, more emergency service and crisis workers on the street, and record investment fought and won from other levels of government.

But the work’s not done.

Each day at City Hall, Olivia Chow fights to make life better for the people of Toronto. Her commitment to us and her love for our city have driven her for decades. As trustee, councillor, MP and now Mayor.

This fall, she's running to continue her work building a more affordable, safe and caring city.

Olivia Chow has dedicated her life to serving the people of Toronto. She’s always been in our corner and she always will be.

— Team Olivia Chow

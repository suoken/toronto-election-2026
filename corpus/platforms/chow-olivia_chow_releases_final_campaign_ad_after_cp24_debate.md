TORONTO (October 22, 2026) – Tonight, Olivia Chow took the stage at the CP24 Municipal Debate. She spoke for Torontonians who are working harder than ever and still falling behind. That includes commuters paying more every week, parents facing higher grocery bills, seniors on fixed incomes and renters worried about the next increase.

Today, Chow released her final ad of the campaign (watch here). In the ad, Chow makes the choice in front of Torontonians clear: do we keep moving forward, or do we risk going backwards?

At the debate, Olivia Chow shared her practical plan to make life more affordable. She will cap TTC fares after 10 rides a week, saving regular riders about $300 a year. Her school lunch program will save families up to $1,900 a year. She will take on grocery price gouging, support seniors, stand up for renters and start construction on 10,000 new homes next year.

This election is a choice. It is a choice between keeping libraries open or closing them, protecting transit service or cutting it, and building affordable housing or walking away from it. Brad Bradford has proposed nearly $6 billion in new spending, including 1,000 new police officers, without saying how he would pay for it. Tonight, voters still didn’t get an answer. When the math doesn’t add up, residents pay through deep cuts to the services they rely on.

Torontonians deserve to know where their mayor stands. Olivia Chow has been clear about whose side she’s on: renters, seniors, transit riders and working families. With four days to go, she will keep asking Torontonians to join her in moving Toronto forward on October 26.

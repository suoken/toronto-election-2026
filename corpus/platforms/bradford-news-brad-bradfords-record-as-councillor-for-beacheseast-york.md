Title: Brad Bradford’s record as councillor for Beaches–East York
Published: 2026-10-23
Issue category: Toronto Mayoral Campaign 2026

For eight years, I’ve had the honour of representing Beaches–East York at City Hall. We’ve worked with neighbours on homes, community spaces and the streets you use every day. Here’s some of that work.

Homes with support

At Trenton and Cedarvale, 59 affordable supportive homes opened in November 2022. WoodGreen runs the building for seniors leaving homelessness or living in insecure housing. We worked with neighbours and our government partners to help make it happen. (1)

Places our community uses

At Topham Park, Council approved my motion for additional clubhouse funding in March 2023. The clubhouse improvements followed in 2024. (2,3)

In 2021, the City, Province and federal government announced $4.9 million for Ted Reeve Arena upgrades. The funding was for the refrigeration system and rink. (4)

The Dawes Road Library rebuild is underway, with a new library, Youth Hub and City community hub. Toronto Public Library expects it to open in 2028. (5)

Street by street

On Woodbine Avenue, I moved changes that Council approved in May 2021. They included a 40 km/h limit, an additional northbound lane at afternoon rush hour and a direction for more physical separation for cycling. (6)

Nobody knows your street better than you do. Your input should carry weight in local decisions.

Recognizing our neighbours

In 2019, I moved the motion to locate the community’s International Mother Language Day monument, the Shaheed Minar, in Dentonia Park. Council approved it. (7)

In July 2023, Council adopted my motion asking staff to work with community organizations to explore a Banglatown Cultural District along Danforth Avenue, between Main Street and Pharmacy Avenue. (8)

The trust of our neighbours

In 2018, I was elected by 288 votes. In 2022, Beaches–East York re-elected me with 15,169 votes, or 54.7 per cent. The margin over the next candidate was 8,878 votes. (9,10)

I’m grateful for that trust and for everyone who helped get this work done. As mayor, I’ll bring that same commitment to every neighbourhood.

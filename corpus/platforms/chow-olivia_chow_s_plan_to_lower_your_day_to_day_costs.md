TORONTO (October 22, 2026) - This election is about affordability, and Olivia Chow is the only candidate that will lower day-to-day costs for people. She can be trusted to deliver practical, tangible savings on things people need each day.

“Every day I hear from people who are doing everything right and still falling behind. They’re putting groceries back at the checkout, skipping trips because the fare adds up, and wondering where they can take their kids that doesn’t cost a fortune,” said Chow. “My plan is about the costs you feel every single day. Cheaper transit, lower grocery bills, and more free places for families. That’s how we build a city people can afford to live in.”

“This election is a choice. Do we move forward, making the city more affordable? Or do we go back to the bad old days of big cuts to public services, closing libraries and raising transit fares,” said Chow. “Your city government has a role to play in reducing your costs.”

Olivia Chow’s plan will cut your day-to-day costs:

-

Lowering grocery bills: More competition by cutting taxes for independent grocery stores, stopping big chains from price gouging and feeding more kids in school.

-

Lowering transit costs: 10 rides per week then free, while investing in better service and freezing fares.

-

Protecting libraries and more kids programming: Deliver free high-quality kids’ programs in all libraries on weekends, so there is always somewhere affordable and engaging for families to go.

-

Supporting seniors so they can age at home, with more homemakers services, mobile health clinics and social opportunities.

-

Protecting Renters by cracking down on bad landlords and making sure apartments are repaired.

-

Building more homes people can afford with 15,000 homes under construction now and 10,000 more breaking ground next year. Lowering rents through more supply.

With the election four days away, Chow continues to travel to each corner of the city to talk to residents about how she will deliver a more affordable city by lowering their day-to-day costs.

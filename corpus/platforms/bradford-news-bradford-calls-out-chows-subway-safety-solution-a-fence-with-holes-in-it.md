Title: BRADFORD CALLS OUT CHOW’S SUBWAY SAFETY SOLUTION: "A FENCE WITH HOLES IN IT"
Published: 2026-08-29
Issue category: Toronto Mayoral Campaign 2026

Mayoral candidate Brad Bradford today released a video with this message: "Sixteen years. That’s how long City Hall has been studying subway platform doors. And a fence with holes is what they finally built," said Bradford. "Is Olivia Chow joking? Because frankly, this so-called solution is embarrassing and Toronto deserves better.”

"A fence, with a gap everywhere a train door lands," Bradford says. "You can walk right through it. It doesn’t stop a fall. It doesn’t stop a delay. It’s not even trying."

The TTC’s 2025 feasibility study rated full height platform edge doors the safest and most secure option available, recommended a genuine pilot at TMU Station, and found the doors would deliver 92 million dollars a year in avoided deaths and injuries and 16 million dollars a year in reduced passenger delays, more than paying for themselves.

At the TTC board’s June 2025 meeting, the board chair moved to delete that pilot from the plan. In March, Bradford brought a motion at City Council to accelerate real platform door barriers across the network. In May, weeks before the election campaign, the fence was announced instead. TTC staff first recommended platform barriers in 2010, with installation to be complete by 2015.

Bradford has committed that as mayor he will end the fence pilot, fund the full height platform edge door pilot at TMU Station that the city’s own study priced and recommended, set a phased installation plan for the busiest Line 1 stations where automatic train control is already in place, and publish timelines, costs and progress so the doors can never again be quietly deleted from an agenda.

"Listen, Toronto isn’t broken. It’s stuck, with projects like this from a Mayor who doesn’t get it," said Bradford. "On October 26th, that’s yours to change. Let’s build the real thing."

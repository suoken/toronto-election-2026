Toronto Candidate Policy Comparer

Side-by-side answers about the 2026 mayoral candidates, built only from cited public sources.

It compares. It never recommends.

Answers are composed from the corpus (campaign platforms, the TVO debate transcript, the council voting record). A claim with no citation behind it does not ship — and when a candidate has no position in the corpus, the comparer says “not addressed” instead of guessing.
